## Scopul
WindowServer + compozitor Quartz pentru FreeBSD (repo `misc/wsbsd`, git root = `/Users/alxb421/Desktop/app/misc`):
- Să compilezi **aplicații Cocoa open-source nemodificate** de pe GitHub pe FreeBSD, care să arate ca pe Mac. Fără binare Apple rulate, fără shim Mach. UIKit e în afara scopului, SwiftUI mai târziu.
- Server SkyLight 1:1 (protocol Mach reconstruit din decompilarea `SkyLight` cu REA/Ghidra), compozitor (software + GL/KMS), blur/Material/Liquid Glass, ferestre (resize, forme), input complet, multi-display/Spaces.
- AppKit peste GNUstep cu back-end propriu `appkit/back-wsd` și temă macOS.
- ISO FreeBSD cu layout macOS (`iso/`), construit local în VM și pornit în QEMU.
- Dock/bară de sus identice cu Mac (decompilate), panourile Open/Save ca pe Mac.
- Activele (`.car`, icns) se folosesc **direct la runtime** din fișierele Mac, niciodată copii PNG randate (utilizatorul a refuzat explicit). Binarele/activele Apple sunt permise pentru teste și active.
- Utilizatorul vrea **implementări reale decompilate, nu placeholdere** care umflă procentele.

## Menu glass lens: measured with a striped backdrop (2026-10-28)
A borderless window of 24 px black/white vertical stripes behind the Mac's Edit menu (tools were in /tmp/lg, not kept: Swift NSWindow with a stripe view at normal level, then the menu opened through System Events, screencapture, a row profile through the menu). Findings: the menu shows the blurred stripes (σ≈4.5 fits the interior), a 1 px bright rim, and at the edges the content is NOT the straight blur: on the right edge the black beyond the edge shows through about 4 px inside the edge although the white stripe goes on 3.5 px past it; on the left the transition appears 1.5 px closer to the edge than the backdrop's; the interior far from the edges has no shift. A one-parameter family `shift = A sin(π d / L)` along the outward normal fitted best at A≈12–14, L≈4–6 (error 3.4 against 4.5 without a lens; the left edge is not explained: the Mac's menu seems asymmetric — a darker inner band on the right/bottom edges is possible). Defaults changed to A=12, L=4.5 (blur.c, the GL shader; WS_MENU_AMP / WS_MENU_L). Open: the asymmetry (measure top/bottom edges with a horizontal-stripe backdrop), the contrast of the fill (the Mac's black stripe 46 / white 140 of 255, ours 31 / 154 in the VM test), the shadow.

Menu glass, horizontal stripes (2026-10-28): the column through the Edit menu at x=250 with 24 px horizontal bands (white [46,70), black [70,94) …; the menu's top rim at y=31): the first ~15 px under the top edge are BRIGHT (115–120) although the backdrop there is black (white starts at y=46): near the top edge the menu shows content from about 14 px further down (a lens sampling inward at the top), whereas at the left edge the shift is ~1.5 px inward and at the right edge the black from outside shows through (outward ~9). A single outward-normal sine (the model in blur.c) does not describe this: the Mac's lens looks direction dependent (stronger inward at the top). Next measurements: a vertical gradient and a checkerboard backdrop to fit a 2-D displacement field per edge; the bottom edge could not be read in this capture (a row of the menu below the rim: shadow or a second panel).

Menu glass lens, model replaced (2026-10-28): a displacement along a FIXED vector fitted much better than the outward-normal sine: vertical stripes (left+right edges) error 1.46 with +x 12 px over a band of 8 px (against 3.4 for the normal model), horizontal stripes (top edge) +y 16–20 over 14 px (3.0 against 7.3 without a lens). So the Mac's menu samples the backdrop from a point displaced to the bottom right by (12, 18) · sin(π d / 11), d the distance from the nearest edge (inward at the top/left, outward at the bottom/right); blur.c and the GL shader use that (WS_MENU_AX, WS_MENU_AY, WS_MENU_L). Not yet compared at the bottom edge or at the corners, and the fill contrast/shadow are still open.

Menu glass lens, per edge (2026-10-28, striped backdrops incl. the bottom edge): left/right/bottom ≈ 12 px over 8 px (bottom fit error 0.19), top ≈ 18 px over 14 px; x to the right and y down (inward at top/left, outward at bottom/right): blur.c and the GL shader now take the distances to the four edges of the glass rectangle (shader: y is up, so the bottom edge's shift is negative). The fill: the Edit menu of the menu BAR shows 46 (black stripe) / 140 (white stripe) of 255 where the context-menu fit (23 backdrops) gives ≈ 31 / 154 — a menu-bar menu may be a different material from a context menu (to check with the same stripes behind a context menu). Corners are not compared.

Menu glass fill vs the backdrop, striped test with a Dock context menu (2026-10-28; Swift window of 24 px vertical stripes at level "floating" over everything but the Dock, the Terminal tile's menu opened with AXShowMenu): black stripe 59, white stripe 140 (median over the rows, away from text); the same stripes behind the menu-bar Edit menu gave 46 / 140; our model (f(L) = 0.1213 + 0.4825 (2L − L²) fitted on 23 natural backdrops) gives ≈ 31 / 154. So (a) the response of the glass at the extremes is flatter than the fit (black 0.23, white 0.55 of full scale), and (b) it depends on more than the local blurred backdrop: the menu near the top (dark menu bar above it) is darker than the one at the bottom — an adaptive large-scale term (a second, much wider blur of the backdrop) is probable. Not implemented: needs a set of backdrops with different large-scale averages (half-black/half-white halves, stripes at different periods) to fit f(L_local, L_wide).

Menu glass tone (2026-10-28): uniform grey backdrops 0/64/128/192/255 behind a Dock context menu (Swift window "floating", AXShowMenu): menu fill 32/82/122/143/146 of 255; the glass is a cubic of the blurred luminance now (f = 0.12494 + 0.86553 L − 0.24983 L² − 0.16865 L³, max error 0.8/255 on these points), flatter at the top than the earlier fit (154 for white). With stripes the black stripe is 59 (not the 32 of f(0)): the Mac's blur behind the glass is wider than σ 4.5 in the interior (σ≈9 would explain the amplitude) while its edges stay sharp (the lens band) — a two-scale blur is probable; not yet changed (the blur radius is shared with other materials).

Menu glass blur (2026-10-28): fitted on the stripes behind a Dock context menu (interior and edge rows, tone = the cubic above): σ 6–8 fits best (error 91 vs 202 for σ 4.5 with the lens; at σ 9 the black stripe is too light); σ=7 now in blur.c and in the GL blur (rad 21, reach 22). VM check with stripes behind the menu-bar Edit menu: black 50 / white 148 (Mac: 46–59 / 140). The lens does not matter for the left rim of that dataset (the stripe boundary sits on the rim), it still stands on the other edges.

Menu shadow (2026-10-28): measured over a white backdrop (Swift window grey 255, a Dock context menu): darkness outside the left edge 29/255 at 2 px, 22 at 4, 17 at 6, 11 at 8, 7 at 10, 5 at 12, 3 at 14, 1 at 16; weaker above (16 at 2 px) than at the sides, a 1 px dark ring (33). Shadow style 2 in the server (`shadow_on == 2`, set by `wsdMenuGlass`): sigma 8, density 0.285, offset 2, outer ring 0.87: the VM shows 228/234/240/245/248/251/253/254 at the same distances. The interior over white is 146 as on the Mac. Not measured: the shadow at the corners and below the menu.
