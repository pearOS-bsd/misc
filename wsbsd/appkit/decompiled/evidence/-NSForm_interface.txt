# : NSMatrix. A single-column matrix of NSFormCell rows. addEntry: appends a titled NSFormCell row (returns it); insertEntry:atIndex:/removeEntryAtIndex:; cellAtIndex:/indexOfCellWithTag:/indexOfSelectedItem; the set* bulk setters (titleAlignment/titleFont/textAlignment/textFont/bezeled/bordered/entryWidth/writing direction) apply to every form cell. Drawing (calcSize/drawCellAtIndex:), the field editor (selectTextAtIndex:) and layout are stage2.
